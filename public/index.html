Collateral is a second exit

Recovery assumes immediate default at origination; gross forced-sale value minus 10% realization costs. Excludes timing, taxes and senior claims. Validate ownership, liens, title transfer, location and resale value.

Expected loss: assumptions, not a rating

PD is judgmental and uncalibrated. EAD equals initial principal; no IFRS 9 staging, lifetime model, macro weighting or approved rating scale is claimed.

48-month repayment profile

Equal monthly payments, reducing-balance interest, no balloon. Rate is annual nominal / 12. Excludes fees, VAT, residual value and exact lease accounting.

CONDITIONAL RESTRUCTURE / NOT AN APPROVAL

Cap the facility at Rp16bn.

Require Rp14bn customer equity for the Rp30bn asset package. At 13% / 48 months, base DSCR improves to 1.83×. Downside DSCR is 1.26×, but falls to 1.00× in the first year after slower collections.

The resize reduces operating risk. It does not remove the need for working capital. Separately fund a three-payment reserve and at least Rp1.43bn for the modeled collection shock. Verify the source of equity and cash, without counting the same funds twice.

Severe-case coverage is 0.75× even after resizing. If minimum volumes, project continuity or equity cannot be verified, defer or decline.

Conditions before disbursement

  1. Reconcile audited accounts, bank statements, tax filings and all existing obligations; establish group-wide debt service.
  2. Verify ownership, SLIK and bureau results through authorized channels; resolve related-party exposure and adverse history.
  3. Validate at least 48 months of enforceable work, tariff and minimum-volume protections, mine permissions and client payment capacity.
  4. Complete a project visit: equipment suitability, uptime, maintenance, fuel logs, HSE, access and redeployment options.
  5. Verify fresh equity, unrestricted liquidity, insurance and legal enforceability of collateral and collection controls.

05 / INTERVIEW WALKTHROUGH

Show your judgment in five minutes.

Download interview guide

English · opening narrative

“My background is in engineering and data analysis. I built this independent equipment-finance case to demonstrate how I would translate operating assumptions into a credit decision. I am moving into credit analysis; this is a learning project, not a transaction I approved.”

  1. 0–1 min: Explain the asset, customer and Rp21bn request.
  2. 1–2 min: Walk from billed hours to CFADS and 1.39× coverage.
  3. 2–3 min: Switch to downside: coverage drops below 1.0×.
  4. 3–4 min: Resize to Rp16bn; explain the separate DSO liquidity gap.
  5. 4–5 min: Close with diligence, conditional structure and early warnings.

Bahasa Indonesia · inti rekomendasi

“Saya tidak akan merekomendasikan pembiayaan Rp21 miliar hanya karena DSCR base case mencapai 1,39 kali. Pada skenario downside, DSCR turun menjadi 0,96 kali. Saya akan mengevaluasi plafon Rp16 miliar, dengan verifikasi kontrak, sumber modal, arus kas, serta perlindungan likuiditas.”

“DSCR yang cukup belum menjamin likuiditas aman. Ketika pembayaran customer melambat, kebutuhan modal kerja naik. Pada plafon Rp16 miliar, DSCR downside tahun pertama hanya sekitar 1,00 kali setelah tambahan piutang. Karena itu dana cadangan dan sumber likuiditas harus diuji terpisah.”

What I would investigate next

Actual borrower accounts; monthly bank reconciliation; corporate debt service; fuel invoices; client creditworthiness; contractor permits; equipment appraisal; repossession cost and timeline; local legal review.

Model boundaries

Borrower, financial screening, project terms, tariffs, debt pricing, PD, LGD and covenant levels are illustrative. The model is deterministic and assumes constant monthly operations.

It does not replace underwriting, a verified repayment history, project survey, legal review, a calibrated risk model or KAF policy. Diesel and coal benchmarks, actual financial statements and corporate debt service are missing.
